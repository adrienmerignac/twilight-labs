# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making Twilight Labs respectful, welcoming, and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully.
- Welcome corrections.
- Challenge ideas, not people.
- Explain claims with evidence where possible.
- Acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect privacy in submitted media.
- Accept that hypotheses may be refuted.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language
- Deliberate misinformation
- Publishing private information without consent
- Fabricating evidence
