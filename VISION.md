# Vision

## Problem

Theorycrafting communities often rely on intuition, copied builds, incomplete guides, undocumented tests, or mechanics imported from similar games.

## Vision

Twilight Labs transforms raw game observations into structured, traceable, and testable knowledge.

Its first supported game is Ragnarok: Twilight Global, while the underlying research tools should remain reusable.

## Mission

### Collect
Capture manual observations, screenshots, videos, OCR outputs, combat measurements, and player profiles without altering the source.

### Understand
Normalize game-specific labels and values into structured data.

### Analyze
Compare profiles, design experiments, evaluate hypotheses, and study formulas.

### Share
Expose knowledge through a web application, research documents, comparison tools, exports, and a future API.

## Success criteria

A player should be able to understand where a claim comes from, how it was tested, how confident we are, what remains unknown, and how to reproduce or challenge it.
